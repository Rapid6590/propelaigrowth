Research behind the example changes

“My process doesn’t have these steps.”

Right. These are examples, not templates. At the second station of our full-day workshop, you map your own process in four to seven steps and circle the one or two that are most stuck.

Change only those one or two at a time, and watch one number for two weeks. Improving every step takes several rounds.
