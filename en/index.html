02

Metrics are outcomes. Behavior is what you can actually work on.

Every number a company watches — revenue, adoption, repeat purchase — sits at the end of a chain. Somewhere in that chain, a specific person has to do a specific thing at a specific moment, and whether they do it depends a great deal on what they see in front of them, how many steps it takes, and what happens by default.

Environmentwhat they see, how many steps, what the default is
▶
Behaviorwho, at what moment, does which action
▶
Metricrevenue, adoption rate, repeat purchase

Behavior is not decided by the person alone. A large part of it is decided by the environment, and the environment is something you can arrange in advance.
